\section{Conclusion}

This template provides a complete IEEE-style starting point for a cryptography course paper. It includes the document class, package configuration, author and abstract structure, modular section files, bibliography setup, examples of common technical elements, and local compile instructions. The included content is deliberately replaceable, but it is long enough to demonstrate how a six-page paper behaves under the selected formatting.

The main practice encouraged by the template is separation of concerns. Global formatting belongs in \texttt{main.tex}, prose belongs in section files, citations belong in \texttt{references.bib}, and generated artifacts should stay out of version control. With that structure in place, a writer can focus on the actual cryptographic contribution while preserving a consistent paper format.
